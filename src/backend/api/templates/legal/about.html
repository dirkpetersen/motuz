Motuz

A web interface for large data transfers between on-premise storage and the cloud.

Motuz lets researchers and staff move their own data between the storage systems they already have access to: the file systems of this site and cloud storage such as Amazon S3, Azure Blob Storage, Google Cloud Storage, OpenStack Swift, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive and SharePoint, Dropbox, SFTP and WebDAV. It is operated by {% if operator.name %}{{ operator_name() }}{% else %}the administrator of this installation{% endif %} and runs the open-source tool rclone on the server to do the actual work.

Sign in to Motuz with your account of this site.

What you can do with Motuz

  • Browse your folders on this site and in your cloud storage side by side, in two panes.
  • Copy files and folders between them, by selecting them or dragging them from one pane to the other. Transfers run on the server, so they continue when you close the browser, and you can follow their progress or stop them.
  • Verify transfers with integrity checks that compare checksums of source and destination.
  • Connect cloud storage once: sign in with Google or Microsoft, or use credentials from your home directory, and use the connection for all later transfers.

Why Motuz asks for access to your Google Drive

When you choose Sign in with Google, Motuz requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. Motuz uses it only to do what you ask for:

  • to list the files and folders of your My Drive or a shared drive when you open it in a pane,
  • to read files you copy from Google Drive to another location and to write files you copy into Google Drive, and to create the folders you create there,
  • to compute checksums when you start an integrity check.

Narrower scopes, which only cover files that Motuz itself created or you opened with it, cannot give access to the existing folders you want to transfer, which is why the full Drive scope is needed. Motuz does not access your Drive in the background, does not scan, analyze, sell or share your data, does not use it for advertising or to train AI/ML models, and only sends files to the destination you choose. The OAuth token is stored on this server only, is never shown in the browser, and is deleted when you delete the connection. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Motuz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
